## How to read the mockup

The mockup is a visual concept. The plan decides what is built.

- The sample numbers in the mockup ("10 users tested", "8 found the plan helpful (80%)", "Average rating 4.2 / 5") and the saved sessions with dates in 2025 are placeholders. They will NOT be used in the real app.
- The "Testing evidence" strip in the mockup is decoration. The real app only shows ratings that real people gave.
- The mockup shows a sidebar (Planner, Marketing, Settings) and a free "Ask anything" box. The real page keeps the existing navbar, asks the 3 intake questions one at a time, and refuses unsafe or off-topic messages.
- The mockup human checkpoint says "needs a quick review from our team". The real app does not claim a team reviews messages. It points to real people and services (trusted person, university student services, Linea de la Vida 800 911 2000, 911 for emergencies).
