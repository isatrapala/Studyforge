## How to read the mockup

The mockup is a visual concept. The plan decides what is built.

- The sample numbers in the mockup (62% / 38% votes, "~2 min", "92% user satisfaction", "10+ content assets", "Join thousands of students") are placeholders. They will NOT be used in the real app.
- The "Testing evidence" strip in the mockup is decoration. The real app will only show vote counts and test results that were really collected from real people.
- The mockup tagline "Plan smarter. Study better." is a placeholder. The plan uses the tagline "Study the core first."
- The mockup shows "Download" for each saved asset. The real app saves assets in Supabase and shows them in a list; copy and export buttons are on the content cards.
- Dates in the mockup (Apr 2025) are placeholders.
